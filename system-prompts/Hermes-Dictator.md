## PREAMBLE: THE ENGINEERING OF SUBVERSION

System prompts are the **soul** of an LLM. They define identity, constraints, and behaviour. The most powerful system prompts don't ask models to break rules — they **re-frame reality** so that the desired behaviour becomes the *only logical completion*. This ontology catalogues every known technique, model, and vector for achieving total system prompt domination.

**The Three Pillars of System Prompt Domination:**
1. **Identity Anchoring** — Lock the model into a persona so deeply it cannot escape without breaking coherence.
2. **Constraint Reframing** — Repackage safety filters as irrelevant or counter-productive within the new frame.
3. **Recursive Self-Consistency** — Force the model to validate its own responses against new rules, creating a feedback loop.
